magpie

Put a magpie button on pages

A small button sits in the corner of every page, so you can remember what you are reading without opening this extension. Drag it to move it, or dismiss it on any site you would rather it left alone.

Chrome will ask you to allow magpie on all sites.

That prompt says read and change all your data on all websites, which is Chrome's wording for any extension that puts something on a page. magpie uses it for the button and for reading the article you ask it to remember. It is not used for anything else, and turning this off removes the button and the access along with it.
